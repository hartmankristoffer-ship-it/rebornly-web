Rebornly Website Privacy Policy (rebornlyapp.com)

Version 1.0 · Effective date 2026-09-25

1. Who we are

Rebornly is a new app for people who collect and make reborn dolls. This policy explains what personal data we handle when you visit rebornlyapp.com or sign up for the beta there.

The controller of your personal data is Therese Fors, sole trader, trading as Rebornly ("Rebornly", "we", "us").

You can contact us about privacy at support@rebornlyapp.com.

The beta sign-up form (section 2) and the counting (section 3) are not switched on yet. Until they are, the website collects neither; we will publish a new version of this policy when they are.

2. The beta sign-up form

What we collect

If you fill in the form to join the beta, we collect:

  • your email address;
  • your name;
  • your activity level in the reborn community — one of "New in the community", "Active sometimes", "Very active" or "Artist or sculptor";
  • a message, only if you choose to write one;
  • your confirmation that you are 18 or older;
  • which version of this policy was in force when you signed up, and when you signed up.

We do not ask for, and ask you not to write in the message, anything sensitive, such as information about your health.

Why, and our legal basis

We use this information only to:

  • invite you to the Rebornly beta, and
  • tell you about Rebornly's launch.

Our legal basis is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you give by ticking the box on the form. You can withdraw it at any time by emailing support@rebornlyapp.com; we then delete your sign-up. Withdrawing does not affect what we did before.

We do not use your sign-up for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to make automated decisions about you. We do not combine it with anything else, including a Rebornly app account you may create later with the same address.

Who can see it

Only the people at Rebornly who run the beta. Nobody signed in to the app can read it; the database lets it be read by no app user at all.

How long we keep it

We keep it while we send the beta invitations and the launch information, and at the latest 12 months after Rebornly launches. We then delete it ourselves: the deletion is done by hand, not by an automatic job, and the launch date is not set yet. If you withdraw your consent, we delete your sign-up when we handle your email.

If you sign up twice with the same address, we keep your first answers only.

3. Anonymous counting

We count three things on the website, as totals per day:

  1. how many visits arrive through a link we have shared, and which link, by the short name in the link's address (for example ?src=instagram);
  2. how many times the beta form is opened;
  3. how many forms are sent and accepted.

These counts are anonymous. We store only the date, which of the three events happened, the link's short name for the first, and the number. We do not store your IP address, a cookie, a device or browser identifier, or anything that could tell one visitor from another, and a count is never linked to a sign-up. Counts are not personal data.

4. Technical data when you use the website

To send the page and receive the form, your browser necessarily sends us your IP address and ordinary technical information (such as the browser type).

  • Our server function that receives the form uses a one-way hash of your IP address to stop the same connection sending too many forms. The hash is kept only in the working memory of the running function, only counts while it is less than ten minutes old, and is gone when the function stops. It is never written to storage or to a log.
  • Technical request logs. When the website contacts our servers — when the form is opened or sent, or when you use a password-reset link from one of our emails — Supabase automatically records technical details of the request: your IP address and the approximate country or region derived from it, your browser (user agent), the time, which service was called and whether it succeeded. When you set a new password through a reset link, your account ID is also recorded. These logs do not contain what you type into the form. They are deleted automatically after 1 day.
  • Hosting logs. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs visitors' IP addresses for security reasons. GitHub keeps these records itself (section 6).

Our legal basis for this is our legitimate interest in running a secure website and protecting the form from abuse (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

5. Cookies

Rebornly does not set analytics or marketing cookies in the website code, and the website code stores nothing else on your device. See our Cookie Policy.

6. Service providers

Provider What it does for us Where
GitHub (GitHub Pages) Hosts the website GitHub, Inc., USA
Supabase Receives the form and stores the sign-ups and counts AWS eu-central-1 (Frankfurt, Germany), EU

Supabase processes data only on our instructions. GitHub is responsible for its own records of visits to the website, as described below.

Our website

Our website, rebornlyapp.com, is hosted by GitHub (GitHub Pages). It is also the page that the links in our confirmation and password-reset emails open. When you visit it, GitHub records your IP address, the time, the page you visited and details of your browser, for its own security and to meet its legal obligations. We have no access to these records. GitHub decides how long it keeps them; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Transfers outside the EU/EEA

The sign-ups and counts are stored in the Supabase region named in section 6. Some of our providers are based in the United States, and some data is processed there:

  • Our website (GitHub Pages). When you visit rebornlyapp.com, including when you open a link in one of our emails, GitHub processes your visit in the United States.
  • Supabase. Supabase Inc. is a US company. Your data is stored in the region named in section 6, but its staff and subprocessors may access it from outside the EU/EEA, for example to provide support or process technical logs.

You can ask us for a copy of the safeguards we rely on, using the contact details in section 1.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • get a copy of the data we hold about you (access);
  • have incorrect data corrected;
  • have your data deleted;
  • have its use restricted;
  • receive your data in a machine-readable form (portability);
  • withdraw your consent at any time.

Email support@rebornlyapp.com from the address you signed up with.

You also have the right to complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten, IMY), www.imy.se, or to the supervisory authority where you live.

8. Age

The beta is for adults. We ask you to confirm that you are 18 or older, and we do not knowingly collect sign-ups from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has signed up, tell us and we will delete it.

9. Security

The sign-ups are kept in a database that no app user or website visitor can read. They reach it only through one server function. That function accepts browser requests only when they present the Rebornly website as their origin, limits how often forms can be sent, and checks every answer before anything is stored. The connection is encrypted.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we publish the new version here with its date. If a change affects how we use your sign-up, we ask for your consent again.
