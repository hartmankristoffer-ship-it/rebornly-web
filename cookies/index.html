Rebornly Website Cookie Policy (rebornlyapp.com)

Version 1.0 · Effective date 2026-09-25

1. What this policy covers

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies, such as local storage, pixels and device fingerprinting, store or read information on your device in other ways. This policy explains how rebornlyapp.com uses them.

2. No analytics or marketing cookies

Rebornly does not set analytics or marketing cookies in the website code, and the website code stores no other information on your device or reads it from there.

  • No advertising or tracking cookies.
  • No analytics cookies. We count visits through our shared links, opens of the beta form and sent forms anonymously, without storing anything on your device and without identifying you. See section 3 of our Privacy Policy.
  • No third-party content that could set its own cookies: the website loads no outside fonts, scripts, videos or social media buttons.
  • Hosting. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security reasons; see section 6 of our Privacy Policy.

Because the website code stores nothing on your device, we do not show a cookie banner or ask for cookie consent.

3. If this changes

If we ever start using cookies or similar technology, we will update this policy first and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent before anything is stored.

4. Controlling cookies in your browser

You can always block or delete cookies in your browser's settings. The website code does not depend on any cookie.

5. Contact

Questions: support@rebornlyapp.com. The controller is named in our Privacy Policy.
